# Customer Churn Dashboard Requirements

This document outlines the design instructions for a Tableau or Power BI dashboard tailored for non-technical business leaders.

## Data Source
Use `data/WA_Fn-UseC_-Telco-Customer-Churn.csv`. For predicted probabilities, export the structured data generated by `src/retention_strategy.py`.

---

## Page 1: Overview (Executive Heatmap)
**Objective:** Provide a 10-second pulse on the health of the customer base.

### Key Metrics (KPI Banner at Top)
- **Total Customers** (Count)
- **Overall Churn Rate** (% of Total)
- **Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)** ($)
- **Total Monthly Revenue at Risk** ($)

### Visualizations
1. **Revenue at Risk by Contract Type (Pie/Donut Chart):**
   - Shows what portion of the $118K at risk comes from Month-to-Month vs 1-Year vs 2-Year.
2. **Churn Distribution (Bar Chart):**
   - Displays count of Churned vs Retained customers.
3. **High-Risk Customer List (Data Table):**
   - Columns: `Customer ID`, `Current Contract`, `Monthly Charges`, `Tenure`, `Predicted Churn Probability`. 
   - Sorted descending by `Predicted Churn Probability`.

---

## Page 2: Churn Drivers
**Objective:** Explain *why* customers are leaving.

### Visualizations
1. **Churn Rate by Tenure Group (Bar Graph):**
   - X-Axis: 0-1 Year, 1-2 Years, 2-3 Years, 3-4 Years, 4-5 Years, 5+ Years.
   - Y-Axis: % Churn Rate.
   - *Insight:* Shows the massive drop-off in churn once a customer hits the 2-year mark.
2. **Churn vs Monthly Charges (Density Plot / Box Plot):**
   - Compare the distribution of Monthly Charges for Churned vs Retained.
   - *Insight:* Highlights that higher-paying customers are churning at higher rates.
3. **Feature Impact Tornado Chart (Horizontal Bar):**
   - Replicate `06_feature_importance.png`.
   - Shows the top positive drivers of churn (Month-to-month, Fiber Optic) vs negative drivers (Tenure, Two-year contract).

## Page 3: Retention Opportunities
**Objective:** Provide an interactive "What-If" simulator for targeted campaigns.

### Interactive Components
1. **Target Segment Filters (Slicers):**
   - Filter by `Contract Type`, `Internet Service`, and `Spending Segment`.
2. **"What-If" Strategy Sliders:**
   - Slider 1: Expected Discount Offered (e.g., 5% to 20%).
   - Slider 2: Estimated Acceptance Rate (e.g., 10% to 50%).
3. **Dynamic KPI Metrics:**
   - **Cost of Campaign:** (Discount * Acceptance Rate * Target Base Monthly Charges).
   - **Revenue Saved:** Derived from customers retained who would have otherwise churned.
   - **Net ROI:** (Revenue Saved - Cost of Campaign).

### Visualizations
- **Waterfall Chart:** Starts with `Total Revenue at Risk`, shows `Campaign Cost` as a negative red bar, `Revenue Saved` as a positive green bar, ending with the `New Revenue at Risk`.
